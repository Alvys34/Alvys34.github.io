About

Designing the decisions players feel.

I’m a game designer focused on systems, economies, and the player behavior that emerges from them. I combine design intuition with live data to make choices that are clear, meaningful, and durable.

How I work

I have shipped live game features for an educational platform serving 50,000+ daily students, including a multiplayer mode, a mission system, and an economy overhaul. My process connects player goals, progression, usability, and measurable outcomes.

Previously, I worked in gameplay and 3C analysis across AAA projects at Amazon Games. I also teach Game Design part-time at Toronto Film School.
